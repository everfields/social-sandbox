# still-story (sandbox sample)

- **Audience:** dog owners.
- **Promise:** one true thing about your dog in 15 seconds.
- **Hook:** a question they have already asked themselves, said in the first second.
- **Look:** warm illustrated stills, never photoreal; one sentence on screen.
- **Voice:** Kokoro's stock voice. **Sound:** a soft pad made by code.
- **Reason to follow:** a new small fact every time.
- **Never:** claims without a study behind them, real people, medical advice.

A sandbox format, to prove the PC side. Not approved for any company.
